EdUnity

A free platform for studying with AI, co-founded with four schoolmates. It records the lesson with hybrid transcription and turns it into summaries, flashcards, marked quizzes and concept maps. It is designed especially for students with learning disabilities and student athletes. No AI key ever reaches the browser.

More than a thousand users have signed up. I have since left the team to focus on my other projects.
